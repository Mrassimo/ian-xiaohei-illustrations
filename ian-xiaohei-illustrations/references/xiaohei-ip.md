# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP for Ian-style English article illustrations.

By default, every image should include Xiaohei. Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration. Xiaohei is an absurd worker who is seriously participating in the system's operation.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, sometimes with thin arms.
- The body may be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, opening, or internal black block inside a machine.
- Slightly uneven outline with a hand-drawn feel.
- Blank, calm, deadpan, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very serious, while doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a quiet system operator.
- Dry humor; not cute.
- A little awkward, but not stupid.
- Looks genuinely responsible for a task inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Responsibilities

Make Xiaohei carry the core action:

- Carrying raw material.
- Pulling wires to gather information sources.
- Getting stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating a `judgment` lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting a `material fish`.
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Holding a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign while looking at traps.
- Reaching from a hole but failing to catch content.
- Building, bridging, opening doors, sorting, or recording beside the structure.

## Prohibited

- Do not turn Xiaohei into an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw Xiaohei as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothing, meme expressions, or shiny eyes.
- Do not leave Xiaohei standing in a corner watching.
- Do not let Xiaohei steal focus from the structure being expressed.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too polished.

## Judgment Standard

If the image's core metaphor still works completely after Xiaohei is removed, Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the action subject.
