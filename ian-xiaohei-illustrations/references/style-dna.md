# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, strange, product-sketch-like, with English handwritten notes and a structure that is clear but not manual-like.

It should feel like an explanation sketch drawn on white paper by someone who has spent years working with AI, product design, development tools, and systems.

## Required

- 16:9 landscape body illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the main visual language: thin line, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector-clean, not heavy outline.
- Generous whitespace: subject takes about 40%-60% of the frame, with at least 35% empty space and preferably one quiet region.
- A few short English handwritten notes: at most 5-8 labels, ideally 1-4 words each.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- The structure should be expressed naturally; do not write the structure type on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line art, character, frames, structure, primary text, and main objects.
- Red: emphasis, problems, emotional points, key reminders, and results.
- Orange: main process, path, arrows, automation flow, or movement from A to B.
- Blue: secondary explanation, mental state, system state, assistant or automation hint.

Blue is not required in every image. Use color sparingly; less is better.

## Absolutely Avoid

- Commercial illustration.
- PPT infographic.
- Formal flowchart.
- Courseware or lesson-slide style.
- Cute cartoon poster.
- Children's illustration.
- Complex architecture diagram.
- Polished flat illustration.
- Tech UI aesthetic.
- Real app screenshot.
- Complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Explaining every node in the image.
- Top-left type titles such as `Workflow`, `System architecture`, `Common traps`, or `Roadmap`.

## Aesthetic Direction

Strange, creative, interesting, clean, and imaginative.

Not cute, childish, complex, or stiff.
